#### Earlier research

- **DNA-based eye-color prediction** (senior research thesis, RPI, spring 2021; supervisor Dr Fan Liu).
  Built multinomial and binary logistic regression models from six pigmentation SNPs, applied them to
  1000 Genomes genotypes, and compared the predictions across populations and with IrisPlex. Intermediate
  eye color was the hardest category to predict.
- **[Genetic and nutritional contributions to height](https://github.com/bleulievre/height-genetics-nutrition)**
  (RPI, 2023). 1000 Genomes variants in height-associated genes combined with country-level height and
  food-supply data; regression, PCA and decision trees.
- **Respiratory outpatient visits and the environment** (2020–2021). Cleaned and linked outpatient,
  air-quality and weather records by visit date, and explored temporal patterns in respiratory visits.
- **COVID-19 campus occupancy** (RPI, summer 2020; supervisor Dr Kristin Bennett). Derived room occupancy
  from course-schedule data and built heatmaps and an R Shiny view for RPI's campus COVID-19 data effort.

The data for the eye-color, respiratory and campus projects belong to the supervising groups or
institutions, so only descriptions are given here.
